Transportation

Taniti visitor access is limited to plane and weekly cruise ship visits. However, once on the island, visitors will have many options for transportation around the island. The following options are available: rental cars, car shares, hotel shuttles, and public transport in the city.

Transportation Costs

Roundtrip airline tickets have a cost range of $150-$250 to get to and from the island.

Cruiseline costs must be checked per the cruiseline's site.

Rental cars are $50 per day.

Car shares are $10 per hour.

Hotel shuttles are $5 per person or included in your hotel stay.

Public transport is $1 per use per person.
