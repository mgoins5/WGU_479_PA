Food & Dining

Taniti has two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores, and one convenience store that is open 24 hours a day.

Also, there are 10 restaurants: five serve mostly local fish and rice, three serve American-style meals, and two serve Pan-Asian cuisine.

Food & Dining Extra Information

Supermarket and grocery store hours are 7am - 10pm while the convenience store is open 24/7.

Restaurants have availability posted outside the establishments as well as menus.
