Experiences

Most people visit Taniti to enjoy the beaches, explore the rainforest, and to visit the volcano. However, there are other things to do, including visiting a local history museum, going on chartered fishing tours, snorkeling, zip-lining in the rainforest, visiting several pubs, including a microbrewery, dancing at a new dance club, seeing a movie, taking helicopter rides, playing at an arcade, visiting art galleries, and bowling. Also, a nine-hole golf course should be operational by next year. Many of these activities are located in Merriton Landing, which is a rapidly developing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay.

Experiences Extra Information

Beaches are accessible 24/7, however, it is only recommended to swim when the flags are green or yellow with a lifeguard present. Lifeguards are stationed between 10am-6pm local time.

Guided tours are available and are $20 per person to explore the forest and see the volcano.

The museum is open from 8am-5pm daily and is free of charge to explore our history!

Fishing tours are available and are $35 per person with the choice to cast and fish yourself.

Helicopter rides to view the island from another perspective are available at $50 per person.

Additional activities will have prices available at the location of the activity.
